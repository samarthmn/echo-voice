Echo VoicePrivacy policy

Support

Email is the only support channel for people who install Echo Voice.

Write to support@example.com.

Replace support@example.com with the real address before this page is published. The placeholder is not a working inbox.

What to include

Describe what you expected, what happened, your macOS version and whether you installed the direct download. If the app could not open an existing library, include the recovery message. Do not attach recordings, transcripts, notes, model-access tokens or a copy of the application-data folder.

What support cannot see

Echo Voice has no account and no hosted meeting service. Audio, transcripts and notes stay on your Mac. There is no analytics or tracking SDK in the app. A support email is the only information you choose to send.

Source and build questions can also use the repository issue templates at github.com/samarthmn/echo-voice. The same rule applies: do not attach private meeting content.
