01 / ONLINE

Bring your meeting app.

Select your meeting application and microphone. Keep separate raw recordings, with optional speakerphone echo processing.

02 / IN PERSON

Put your Mac at the table.

Choose the room microphone and record. Optional local speaker grouping adds labels afterward, so you can review who said what.

Speaker labels are estimates, not verified identities or isolated voice recordings. Overlapping voices may remain uncertain.

Your meeting isn’t
a cloud upload.

Meeting audio, transcripts and notes are processed locally. There is no hosted Echo Voice backend, tracking SDK or cloud inference fallback.

Permission with a purpose

Capture only the inputs you choose. In-person recording uses the microphone. Online recording also needs system-audio access. Visual context and screenshots are not captured.

Retention is your choice

Keep recordings until you delete them. Export independent copies. Back up the library with the app closed. Deleting a meeting does not remove copies you exported.

Privacy details

Explicit setup downloads contact the displayed model hosts, which receive ordinary connection information and apply their own policies. Optional gated-model credentials are used during setup; Keychain storage is optional. Local inference workers do not receive those credentials. Optional Ollama is separately managed by you and connects over loopback.

Echo Voice does not separately encrypt its files; macOS account access and FileVault settings apply. Local diagnostics should be reviewed before sharing. External source, support and model links open sites with their own policies. Full policy and retention guidance are included in the repository.

A few useful details.

What to expect before you install.

Which Macs are supported?

Echo Voice targets Apple Silicon and macOS 14.2 or later. Minimum-system and hardware qualification is still pending for the 1.0 public release. Memory needs depend on the models you select; a larger notes model may need substantially more memory.

Does it work without internet?

After provisioning, meeting processing is designed to run offline. Explicit model setup and updates need downloads. The production offline-network gate remains part of qualification. Ollama requires its own local server, not an internet service.

How much storage does it need?

Model data sizes are shown above and in Settings. Production builds also include executable runtimes. Recording checks an eight-hour storage estimate using actual audio formats, warns below 3 GB free, and stops safely below 1 GB. Eight-hour endurance validation will be performed manually before release qualification.

Will the transcript always be correct?

No. Microphone placement, overlapping speech, background noise and model choice affect results. Review uncertain passages against the original audio, use vocabulary for names and terms, and correct text before relying on it.

Is Echo Voice free and open source?

The planned 1.0 release is free through both channels. Echo Voice's original source is MIT licensed; dependencies and models retain their own terms. The repository includes build and contribution guidance.
