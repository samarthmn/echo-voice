Echo Voice 1.0 — local meeting workspace

Installation: copy Echo Voice into Applications. Local qualification builds
are not notarized public releases; consult the included build status.

Production-channel candidates include speech runtimes. On first launch the
Setup Guide checks the runtime, helps you download a speech model, offers
optional notes and speaker grouping, and shows microphone access. Reopen it
from Help > Setup Guide or the status indicator at the bottom of the sidebar.
Downloads show progress and continue while you use the app; recording starts
after a download finishes or is cancelled. Settings > Models manages models
later. Ollama notes use an already-installed local model; Echo Voice does not
download or remove Ollama models.

Choose New Meeting > Online meeting or In person. In-person mode records
only the room microphone. Online mode preserves selected-app and microphone
tracks separately. Browser capture can include other audible browser tabs.
Input testing is optional. Acknowledge participants' recording consent.

Stop to finish processing. Replay the transcript, correct text and names,
save highlights/bookmarks, review notes and export independent copies.
Speaker labels do not verify identity. Generated text needs review.

Recordings survive local inference failures. Missing models can be installed
later. Keep Echo Voice open while recording and processing. Use the app to
delete meetings; do not modify its data directory while it is running.

Source and guidance: https://github.com/samarthmn/echo-voice
No account, analytics or cloud inference fallback. Model setup needs network
access; provisioned processing is local. Public release qualification remains
tracked separately from the existence of this build.
